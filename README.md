# Gemini Superpower

Supercharge your Gemini experience with folders, instant search, and one-click export.

Gemini Superpower is a Chrome extension that adds a lightweight workspace layer on top of [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) — organize your conversations, find them instantly, and take your content with you.

## ✨ Features

- 📂 **Folders** — organize your Gemini chats into custom folders
- 🔍 **Instant Search** — find any saved conversation as you type
- 📤 **One-Click Export** — export the current chat as **Markdown**, **TXT**, or **JSON**
- 🚀 **Non-intrusive** — lives in a slim sidebar; your Gemini experience stays untouched

## 🧩 Pro

## 📦 Install

1. Install from the [Chrome Web Store](#) *(link coming soon)*
2. Open [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
3. Click the blue sidebar button in the bottom-right corner — done!

## 🔒 Privacy

Gemini Superpower does **not** collect, store, or transmit your conversation content. All data (folders, chat titles, preferences) stays in your browser's local storage. License keys are validated through a secure proxy; no personal data is involved. See our full privacy policy for details.
